# Lengua de 3.º — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Lengua becomes the second subject of Repasitos for 3.º: its syllabus, classes, exercises answered by tapping words or with an on-screen letter keyboard, original stories to read with questions, its own daily session after the maths one, its placement test and its section in the adult report.

**Architecture:** Lengua skills carry the prefix `lng:`; a single helper `subjectOfSkill` lets every reader of mastery/attempts keep the two subjects apart (maths readers filter to maths first, so nothing changes for them). The syllabus, planner, session, placement and classes take a `subject` argument; Lengua reuses the existing models (syllabus blocks/topics/stages without worlds, `clase()` five phases, `ExerciseStep`, `GameHost`) and adds new exercise kinds with a new `text` answer type and a `LetterKeyboard` that follows the numeric `Keypad` + `activePad` pattern.

**Tech Stack:** React 18 + TypeScript, Vite, Dexie, Supabase sync, Vitest + Testing Library, Playwright (4 projects).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-lengua-3-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Child-facing text in Spanish; no inclusive language (masculine generic), only two genders.
- Explain before practising: every grammar, spelling and vocabulary topic has a five-phase class («que no sea aburrido ni solo ejercicios sin explicar»).
- Hints never give the answer, and never leave it one trivial step away.
- Stories are **original** (never copied from books), adventures with animals and humour, Lumi and the child's companion as characters, 120–250 words each.
- Lengua answers with an on-screen letter keyboard, QWERTY español with ñ and accents; a physical keyboard also works. No handwriting of letters in this project.
- Maths must not change: its progress, session, stars, menu, placement, classes and reports behave exactly as before (existing tests keep passing).
- Stars, shop, album and chest are shared; each subject gives its session stars once a day.
- Lengua only for grade 3 or no grade; grade 2 and 4 show «Pronto».
- Never play audio in tests (Playwright mutes it). TTS requests are ≤ 400 characters (`api/tts.ts` MAX_CHARS).
- Server limits: `error_type` and `kind` ≤ 32 characters; `skill` ≤ 64.
- Type check `npx tsc -b`; commits on `dev` with trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. A maths-only child (Lengua off) after this change: same «Hoy», session, Repasar count, report and placement as before (Task 1 and Task 8 tests).
2. A child with both subjects: Lengua skills never appear in the maths warm-up, mix, review or maths report, and the other way round (Task 1 test over the real readers).
3. Typed answers with case, accents and a stray space: «Árbol», «arbol », «ARBOL» — the check rules are explicit and tested (Task 3).
4. A phone at 390 px: the letter keyboard fits under the exercise without hiding the answer box, and no sideways scroll (Task 3 e2e).
5. Flags merge forever (OR): Lengua's placement, session and class flags never collide with maths' (`placement`, `sesion:<date>`, `clase:<topicId>`) (Task 1 test).

### Task 4: Grammar exercises

**Files:** Create `src/engine/lengua/words.ts` (word banks), `src/engine/lengua/grammar.ts`; Modify `src/types/exercise.ts`, `generate.ts`, `check.ts`, `hints.ts`, `describe.ts`, `daily.ts` (`ITEM_SECONDS`, `statementOf`), `src/activities/registry.ts`; Create views `src/activities/views/lengua/TapWordsView.tsx`, `ClassifyView.tsx`, `TransformWordView.tsx` (+ gallery); Tests in `src/engine/__tests__` (generate/check/hints lists) and view tests.

**Kinds (≤ 32 chars):**
- `lng-tap-words`: a sentence; «Toca los sustantivos / adjetivos / verbos / artículos» → Answer `marks`.
- `lng-classify`: one word → choose class, or común/propio, masculino/femenino, singular/plural, presente/pasado/futuro → `choice`.
- `lng-transform`: write the word changed (al femenino, al plural, en pasado…) → `text` with `exact`.
- `lng-sentence-parts`: tap where the subject ends / choose the subject → `marks` or `choice`.
- Word banks: ≥ 60 nouns (with gender/number), 40 adjectives, 40 regular verbs in 3 tenses (3.ª persona), 30 sentences built from them; vocabulary of 3.º, animals and adventures.
- Hints: three levels, from the idea («Un sustantivo nombra personas, animales o cosas») to a guiding question, never the answer.

### Task 5: Spelling and vocabulary exercises, dictation

**Files:** Create `src/engine/lengua/spelling.ts`, `vocabulary.ts`; views `MissingLetterView.tsx`, `DictationView.tsx`, `OrderWordsView.tsx`, `PairWordsView.tsx`; same pipeline files as Task 4.

**Kinds:**
- `lng-missing-letter`: c/qu, g/gu/gü, r/rr, mp/mb, mayúscula inicial → choose the letters (`option`) at level 1, type the whole word (`text`, `exact`) at level 2.
- `lng-punctuation`: a sentence missing its marks → choose/type `. , ¿? ¡!` (`text` `exact` on the sentence or `option` per gap).
- `lng-stress`: tap the stressed syllable (`marks`/`choice`).
- `lng-dictation`: the app says a word or short sentence (TTS, ≤ 400 chars, a «Repetir» button) and the child types it (`text`, `exact`); when voice is off, the view shows a «🔊 Escuchar» button only and never the text.
- `lng-alphabet`: order 3–5 words alphabetically (`marks` order or drag-free tap order).
- `lng-pair-words`: sinónimos, antónimos, familia, aumentativo/diminutivo, compuestas → `choice` or `text`.
- [ ] Step 1: failing tests as in Task 4 plus: dictation never renders the expected text before answering; TTS text ≤ 400 chars. Steps 2–5; commit «Lengua: spelling, vocabulary and dictation exercises».
